About

I am a Solutions Architect at NVIDIA. My work spans the AI stack for quantitative finance, from model and algorithm development to hardware-aware optimization. My recent work explores LLM distillation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), optimizer design, and GPU-accelerated methods for large-scale portfolio and risk modeling. I am particularly interested in identifying quantitative workloads that can benefit from accelerated computing and improving the efficiency of established methods. I have also worked on explainable machine learning, similarity learning, and graph-based methods for financial applications.

Previously, I was a Data Scientist with the Investment AI team at BlackRock, where I built trading, pricing, and liquidity algorithms for fixed-income asset classes and ETF trading. Before that, I was a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Vanguard, building end-to-end machine learning systems.
